CarPlay Video Permission — Simple Steps

For the admin of the Apple Developer account (the account that owns the DrivePlay app).

Why is this needed? Apple only lets a car show video for apps that Apple has approved. So one request has to be sent to Apple from the Apple account, and it includes a legal agreement (the CarPlay Entitlement Addendum) that only the account owner can accept. After Apple approves, the developer does the rest. Nothing else is needed from the admin.

1Open the website

Go to https://developer.apple.com/contact/carplay/

2Sign in

Use the main Apple ID of the account (the one that owns com.rushabh.cartvv). Type the 2FA code that comes to the phone.

3Fill the boxes

BoxWhat to type
App nameDrivePlay
Bundle IDcom.rushabh.cartvv
Version1.2.0
App Store linkApp is still in development, not on the App Store yet
App typeVideo
Which countriesAll countries

4The big text box (app description)

Copy this and paste it:

DrivePlay is an iPhone app that plays live TV and video playlists in the car through CarPlay.

The user adds their own media server or playlist (M3U playlist, Xtream Codes, Plex), and the
app shows the channels as a CarPlay list. Choosing a channel starts that stream in the car's
now-playing area, where the car screen shows the video while the car is parked.

DrivePlay is a player: it does not host, sell or distribute any video. All content comes from
services the user adds themselves, and the user signs in with their own credentials. The app
also supports AirPlay video streaming, so a stream can be sent to an AirPlay display.

CarPlay is used only for browsing the channels and starting playback; every part of the flow
works from the car screen alone.

5If they ask "do you own the content?"

Copy this and paste it (only if it is true of the app):

DrivePlay does not provide video content and holds no content rights. It is a client: the
content is served by the user's own server or subscription, and the app only sends the player
the stream URL that the user's own account returned. There is no catalogue, no library and no
third-party content in the app.

6If they ask for a video showing it working

Record three short parts:

  1. the app on the iPhone,
  2. the same app on the car screen,
  3. one channel playing video on the car screen.

Upload it to Google Drive (or iCloud, or YouTube as an unlisted video) and paste the link.

7Agree and send

  • Tick the box (or open the link) for the CarPlay Entitlement Addendum and accept it. It just says: no games, no social media, and everything must work from the car screen.
  • Press Submit.

8Wait

Apple replies by email within a few days. If they ask any question, forward the question to the developer — the answers above are the technical content. Nothing else to do after submitting.

After Apple says yes — the developer's job (the admin does nothing).
  1. Turn on the “CarPlay Video” switch on the app’s App ID page.
  2. Make a new signing profile and build the app onto the phone.
  3. Done — the car screen shows the channel list and plays the video.
One decision before submitting. App type Video (the new one, needs iOS 27, the car shows the app as a video app) or Audio (older, works in many more cars, may still play video, but the car shows it as a music app). Apple allows only one of the two per app. If unsure, pick Video.
