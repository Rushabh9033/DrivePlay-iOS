# Player features to add next — 2026-11-04

The user asked for more of the kind of thing the **minimised player** is: the
small touches that make a player feel like it belongs on the phone *and* on the
car's screen. Each one says what it is, what it costs, and what it is worth.

## The three that are worth doing first

### 1. "Continue watching" on Home (one afternoon)

YouTube's home screen opens with the videos you half-watched. DrivePlay
already keeps a watch history — it is used for *"watch again"* — but the Home
tab does not show it. The row is a row of thumbnails with a **progress bar**
under each one, and tapping it resumes the channel where it stopped.

*Why first:* everything it needs is already there (`WatchHistoryStore`, the
channel artwork, the resume point in `PlaybackManager`). It is a row in a
list, so the car can have it too — the car's Home tab already has the
*"Show all N channels"* pattern for a row that does not fit.

*Cost:* a new row in `HomeView`, a `PlaybackManager.resume(channel:)` that
seeks to the stored position, and the same row for `CarPlayTabs`.

### 2. A queue — "play next" (a day)

The player takes a **list** of what comes next instead of one channel. The
docked player gets a "▤ next up" strip; when a channel ends, the next one
starts by itself. On the car the strip is not shown (CarPlay draws its own
now-playing screen), but the queue is still played — the picture simply
changes over.

*Why:* the app already knows the **order of a category**
(`PlaylistStore.channels(inGroupOf:)` — that is what the minimised bar's ⏭
plays). The queue is that list, with the current channel taken out.

*Cost:* a `playQueue: [Channel]` on `PlaybackManager`, one method
(`enqueue(_:)`), and the end-of-item hook that starts the next one.

### 3. The mini bar's long-press menu (an hour)

Long-pressing the minimised bar opens a small menu: **Go to channel**,
**Add to favourites**, **Remove from favourites**, **Sleep timer**,
**Playback speed**. YouTube does exactly this, and on a car it is the only way
to reach a control without leaving the video.

*Why first:* the bar is already a view with a button in it — this is a
`Menu` on that button and the five actions that already exist somewhere in the
app (favourites, sleep timer, speed are all one-liners over existing state).

## The ones after that

| Feature | What it is | What it costs |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| **Sleep timer** | The video stops after 15 / 30 / 60 minutes. On a car it is the answer to *"I fell asleep at a red light"* — the engine is off, the video is not. | A timer on `PlaybackManager` + one entry in the menu. |
| **Playback speed** | 0.5× – 2×, remembered per channel. A news channel at 1.25× is a real thing people do. | `AVPlayerItem.audioTimePitchAlgorithm` + `player.rate`. One line to apply, one to remember. |
| **Resume from a timestamp** | *"Watch from 12:34"* — the user marks a moment in a film and finds it again. | A row in `WatchHistoryStore`, a row in the mini-bar menu. |
| **Picture controls** | Brightness and contrast for the car's screen. A car's screen at night is dimmer than the phone's, and the app's own night theme is darker still. | A `CPTemplate` cannot do it; the picture is the system's. It has to be a **player** setting that changes the picture's own contrast — a `CIFilter` on the video output. **Big**: it needs the render path. |
| **Double-tap the mini bar** | Play/pause, like YouTube. | A tap counter on the bar's button. Half an hour. |
| **The car's own voice search** | The microphone feature, driven from the car: the car sends a voice command, the phone listens and plays the result. | The audio session is the car's while CarPlay is up — the phone's own microphone has to be asked for **by name** (a known trap). **Big**, and it needs a human on a real car. |
| **A live guide (EPG)** | *"What's on now"* for the channels that carry one. | The playlists carry no EPG, so it needs a second source. **Big**, and it is a product decision. |
| **Offline for the car** | A channel that is downloaded and played on the car with no phone network. | `LocalMediaService` can already hold a file; the car needs to be able to play it. **Big**. |
| **A bitrate choice for the car** | The car's screen is far, so 4K is a waste of the phone's battery. | The stream URL has to carry the choice. Medium. |

## What was left out, and why

- **A floating picture-in-picture window.** The user was explicit: the
  minimised player is a **docked bar**, not a floating window. A real
  `AVPictureInPictureController` is a different thing and is not asked for.
- **The mini bar inside the tab bar.** It sits **above** it — the user was
  explicit about that too ("the minimise bar should be not cover the main tab
  bar").
