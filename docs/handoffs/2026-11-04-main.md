## Gotchas learned
- A theme made only of dynamic system colours is not a theme — it is the
  phone's setting wearing the app's clothes.
- One plist key beats a root-view modifier: `preferredColorScheme` leaves
  sheets and other windows on the phone's setting.
- The key has to be read from the **running** app in a test; a test that reads
  `project.yml` proves the repository, not the phone.
- The car cannot be told what colour to be.
